Daily routine

10 minute ka morning routine

Khaali pet ya halka kuch khaane ke 2–3 ghante baad, shaant jagah pe karo. Dheere-dheere, apni comfort mein.

  1. Gardan & kandhe ka rotation~1 minGardan dheere side-to-side, phir kandhe aage-peeche ghumao. Jhatka nahi dena.
  2. Tadasana~1 minSeedhe khade hokar haath upar, body ko halka stretch karo, saans normal.
  3. Cat-Cow (Marjariasana)~1–2 minGhutno aur haathon pe aakar, saans ke saath peeth ko upar-neeche. Dheere.
  4. Anulom Vilom3–5 minAaram se baith ke ek-ek naak se saans andar-bahar. Zor nahi lagana, saans rokna nahi.
  5. Bhramari~1 minAankhein band, saans bahar chhodte hue halki "mmm" gunjan. 3–5 rounds.
  6. Shavasana~1–2 minPeeth ke bal let kar poori body ko dheela chhodo. Bas saans ko mehsoos karo.
⚠️ Dhyan rakho: Kahin dard ya chakkar aaye to turant ruk jao. Gardan, kamar ya ghutne ki problem ho to pehle doctor se poochho. Pregnancy, BP, heart ya koi aur condition ho to bina expert salah ke shuru na karein.
Safety disclaimer

Zaroori baat

Shiv Way Yoga ka content sirf general wellness aur education ke liye hai. Yeh kisi bhi bimari ka ilaaj ya medical advice nahi hai.

  • Agar aapko koi health condition hai (BP, heart, sugar, back/knee problem, pregnancy, surgery ke baad etc.), to pehle doctor se consult karein.
  • Hamesha apni comfort limit mein practice karein — dard mein push na karein.
  • Koi bhi discomfort, dard ya chakkar ho to turant ruk jaayein.
  • Hum koi "cure" ya medical result ka promise nahi karte.
